## Objective

Build a modular, open-source platform to test batteries, understand their behavior, and explore recovery.

## Current Status

BatteryLab is an active project. Specifications and measured results will be documented as the work develops.

## Scope

The work spans battery testing and measurement, power-supply integration, serial-connected instruments, and automation. Fixtures and 3D printed components are part of the platform, with analysis and reporting capabilities planned for the future.

This is an ongoing project, with no commercial availability announced.
